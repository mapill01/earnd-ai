"I looked around my school and saw kids glued to their phones with nothing to do and no money. I thought — there must be neighbours nearby who'd pay us to help them out."

Leo is a teenager who saw the problem first-hand — and decided to do something about it. Earnd.ai wasn't designed in a boardroom. It was sketched out by a kid who wanted real purpose, real money, and a reason to step away from a screen.

How It Works

Simple for everyone involved

01
Sign up with a parent
Kids register with parental consent. A parent account is linked from day one — they approve everything before it goes live.
02
Set your services & rates
Choose from an approved job list — gardening, dog walking, car washing, tutoring and more. Set your own rate (minimum £5 per job).
03
Get booked & get paid
Households find and book you. Check in when you arrive, check out when done. Payment goes straight to your parent's account — rated, recorded, real.
01
Create a family account
You register first. Your child is linked to your account. You control their profile, availability, and which jobs they can take on.
02
Approve every job
You get the full household name, address and job details before your child can accept. One tap to approve or decline. No surprises.
03
Track in real time
Check-in and check-out notifications. Earnings go to your bank account. A full earnings history for every job your child completes.
01
Sign up & verify
Register with your address and phone. Households are verified before they can contact any child on the platform.
02
Browse & book locally
Search by job type and area. See ratings, completed jobs, and set rates. Book and pay securely — all in the app.
03
Rate & rebook with ease
Leave a review after every job. Found someone brilliant? One-tap rebook or set up recurring jobs. Build a trusted local relationship.
Child Safety

Safety isn't a feature. It's the foundation.

Every design decision starts with one question: is this safe for a young person? Here's what that looks like in practice.

✅
Parental consent at signup AND every job
Nothing happens without a parent saying yes — not once at registration, but for every single booking.
📍
Full address shared with parents before jobs
Parents see the complete household details — name, address, job type — before approving anything.
🕐
Check-in / check-out tracking
Parents are notified when their child arrives and leaves every job. Real-time peace of mind.
💬
In-app messaging only — AI monitored
No phone numbers or addresses exchanged in chat. AI monitors all messages and images for safety.
⭐
Two-way ratings & flagging
Kids rate households, households rate kids. Either side can flag concerns. Repeated issues result in removal.
📋
Approved job types only
We curate the list of allowed chores. No job type is added without a safety review. Scope creep is blocked by design.
